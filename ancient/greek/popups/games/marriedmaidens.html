Married

Married women who attempted to watch the men's events or even the Maidens' Race could be punished by being thrown off a nearby mountain. The ancient Greek historian Pausanias wrote of a widow named Callipateira who escaped this fate, however. Desperate to watch her son compete at the Olympics, she disguised herself as a male trainer. When she was discovered, the judges freed her out of respect for her father, brothers, and son, all of whom had been victorious there. Following this, however, the judges required all trainers to strip before entering Olympic arenas.
