Volume kappa, number eta

Volume 2 number 8 is expressed using the Greek letters kappa and eta. Number 8 would have been the letter eta using the ancient Greek numeration system. This system used the first 9 letters of a 27-letter Greek alphabet for the numbers 1 through 9. The next 9 letters stood for tens, from 10 through 90. The last 9 letters were symbols for hundreds, from 100 through 900. Arithmetic was almost impossible using this system.
