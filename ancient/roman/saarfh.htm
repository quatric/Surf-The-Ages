We at The Roman Forum are proud to bring you a collection of Web sites celebrating the greatest empire of all time. Once it spanned the three great parts of the world--Europe, Africa and Asia. Now, sadly, the empire is divided into two. As we approach 500 years after the birth of Jesus Christ--founder of our religion--the West Roman Empire is no longer ruled by Romans.

The Roman Forum team is busy reconstructing sites from great moments in our empire's history. First we give you a taste of life when disaster struck at the height of Rome's glory.
