The place for business travelers to meet!

Marvel at the impressive colonnades, bronze and marble statues, and the magnificent Temple of the Capitoline Triad, where visitors may join residents in worshiping Jupiter, Juno, and Minerva.

 

Continue your business or simply relax at one of our beautifully decorated, centrally located baths: the Stabian, the Forum, and the Central. Slip into the hot water of the caldarium. Lounge in the warm steam of the tepidarium, then plunge into the invigorating coolness of the frigidarium. Complete your bath with a visit to our skilled hair-removers, perfumers, and masseurs.

Saltwater baths available at the establishment of M. Crassus Frugi. Clientele restricted.

 

Pamper yourself by staying at our nearly completed luxury hotel, featuring a private spa with thermal baths.

Entertain clients in one of five private dining rooms, elegantly decorated with exquisite wall paintings, fountains, and sumptuous couches.

Savor the hotel's excellent cuisine, featuring
A. Umbricius Scaurus's world-famous garum, made from only the finest tuna, mackerel, and moray eel.

 

Divert yourself or entertain clients at Pompeii's three performance arenas.

• Delight in comedies, dramas, and pantomimes at our 5,000-seat outdoor theater, which adjoins a charming garden for enjoyable walks during intermission.

• Enjoy sophisticated musical performances, poetry recitals, and lectures in the intimate 1,300-seat indoor Odeon.

• Cheer fearless gladiators as they battle each other and wild beasts at the 20,000-seat Amphitheater, large enough for the entire population of Pompeii!
