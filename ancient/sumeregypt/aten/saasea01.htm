The Aten, the Sun God, is the one, universal, all-powerful god. Ancient of days, the Aten is embodied in the disk of the sun with outstretched rays. Each ray ends in a human hand that embraces what the sunlight touches. The Aten is the visible representation of life's creative force.

The Majesty of Akhenaten (bestowed with eternal life) is the human form of the Aten. Only the pharaoh and his family may worship the Aten directly. All other Egyptians, including priests, must send their prayers to the pharaoh, through whom they receive the Aten's good graces.
