Valley of the Kings

The Valley of the Kings is a rocky, narrow gorge that was used as a cemetery by the pharaohs of ancient Egypt between 1550 and 1100 B.C. The Valley, sometimes called the Valley of the Tombs of the Kings, lies on the west bank of the Nile River across from Luxor. Over 60 tombs have been discovered in the Valley of the Kings and in the adjoining Western Valley.
