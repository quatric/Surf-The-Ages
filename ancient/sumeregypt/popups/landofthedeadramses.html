Land of the dead

The land of the dead, also known as the Land of Eternity and the Field of Reeds, was the ancient Egyptians' name for the afterlife. They believed the afterlife was a continuation of one's existence before death. For this reason, food, furniture, clothing, and other items were placed in tombs for the dead person's use in the afterlife. The tombs of some Egyptians also had statues representing servants who would care for them in the next world.
