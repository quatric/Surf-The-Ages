Prince Tutankhaton

The identity of the parents of the legendary Tutankhaton (who changed his name to Tutankhamen after he became the pharaoh) remains a mystery. Some scholars believe he was the son of Akhenaten and Kiya, a lesser wife with the title of "Greatly Beloved Wife." Others have suggested that he was the son of Amenhotep III and, thus, Akhenaten's half-brother. Tutankhamen also became the husband of Ankhsenpaaton, the third daughter of Akhenaten and Nefertiti.
