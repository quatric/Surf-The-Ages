vanquished

In 1285 B.C., in one of the greatest battles of ancient times, Ramses and his Egyptian forces fought the Hittites for land the Hittites had seized from Egypt. The two armies met at Kadesh on the Orontes River, north of Palestine. Ramses claimed a great victory, though, in fact, neither side won. About 1269 B.C., Ramses made a treaty with the Hittite king that divided the disputed land between them and sealed the treaty with his marriage to a Hittite princess.
