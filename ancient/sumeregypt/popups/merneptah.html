Merneptah

Named pharaoh at about age 60, Merneptah was the 13th son of Ramses II, who outlived the first 12 of his sons and heirs. A black granite monument found at Merneptah's funeral temple in 1896 contains the earliest known reference to Israel in Egyptian sources. The inscription boasts that in the fifth year of his reign Merneptah "laid waste" to Israel.
