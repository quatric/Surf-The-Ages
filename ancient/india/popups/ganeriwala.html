Ganeriwala

An Indus city as large as Mohenjo-Daro, Ganeriwala lay about 180 miles (300 kilometers) northeast of Mohenjo-Daro along the Ghaggar-Hakra River (also known as the Sarasvati River). This river, which dried up beginning in about 1700 B.C., once flowed east of, and parallel to, the Indus River.
