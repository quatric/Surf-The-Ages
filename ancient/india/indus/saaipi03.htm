We of Mohenjo-Daro are justly proud of our well-planned and beautifully constructed city, which is about 3 miles in circumference. Many of our buildings sit on platforms of sun-dried or kiln-fired bricks, which provide security against most floods. We also use bricks, created to a standard size, to build our dwellings, shops, and storage and manufacturing facilities.
