As befitting a significant trade and commercial center, Mohenjo-Daro boasts a diverse population that includes representatives of many of the ethnic groups in the Indus Valley Civilization. Our abundant food supplies and thriving mercantile activities ensure that the citizens of Mohenjo-Daro are

  • happy
  • well satisfied with their lives
  • unconcerned with conquest and warfare

Economic classes

Most of our people live a comfortable life. Some people have achieved great success in business and rank as our upper class. Only a few unworthy souls have found it difficult to participate in the general prosperity.

Fashion

Both men and women dress in robes worn over the left shoulder and under the right arm. Both also often wear fillets, many made of gold or silver. Some men wear beards, while others prefer to be clean-shaven. Their hair may be short or long.

Women adorn themselves with lovely jewelry made from locally produced beads as well as from imported stones. They wear their hair in a variety of styles, from simple braids and coils to elaborate creations with cascading curls.

Occupations

Most of the citizens of Mohenjo-Daro are farmers, traders, or craftworkers. The city boasts a large and highly skilled artisan community. Internationally renowned, these artists create beautiful and useful objects for everyday life as well as luxury goods for the discriminating.

Leaders

Our city leaders and priests, though fewer in number, ensure that our city runs smoothly and that our civic and spiritual needs are met.
