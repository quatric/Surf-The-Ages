Our houses are comfortable, with thick walls and high ceilings to keep residents cool in our hot climate. A typical home measures 30 feet by 27 feet and has four or five rooms. The rooms surround a central courtyard. Windows always overlook the courtyards, never the streets. Most homes have a staircase, which leads to a second story or flat roof.

Almost every home has a room for bathing, and many are equipped with a latrine. Both are served by the city’s efficient drainage system.
