Welcome to the city of Mohenjo-Daro. Our great city lies near the west bank of the Indus River–King River, giver of life. Surrounded by fertile fields, we are home to about 40,000 people.

We are proud to be part of a vast cultural and trading confederacy, linked to other great cities in the Indus Valley by:

  • a mutual language
  • common culture
  • shared spiritual beliefs
  • strong economic ties

We of the Indus Valley make up the world’s largest urban civilization, consisting of some 1,500 cities, towns, and villages. Our confederacy covers 400,000 square miles, twice the area controlled by the empires of either Sumer or Egypt.

Whether you’re a first-time visitor or a regular traveler to Mohenjo-Daro, you’re sure to find that our city offers every comfort and convenience you might desire. Stay a week or stay forever, Mohenjo-Daro will win a place in your heart.
