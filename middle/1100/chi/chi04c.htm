Other pathways to the Civil Service

Besides earning a chin-shih degree, there are three other ways to enter the Civil Service:

• Protection. Officials of sufficient rank may appoint one or more relatives to Civil Service posts. This way of entry is known as protection.

• Military service. Military personnel may transfer from military service to Civil Service.

• Facilitated examinations. Older men who have failed the other examinations several times may enter the lower ranks of the Civil Service by passing an easier exam, called a facilitated exam.
