3. New European universities founded

Three of Europe's leading cities, Paris, France, and Bologna, Italy, developed universities in the past century. In England, an important university began to develop in a town called Oxford. This minor center of learning gained prominence when hostility between England and France forced English students to pursue their education at home rather than at the University of Paris.
