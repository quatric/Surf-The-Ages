2. Minamoto family wins a civil war in Japan

The Minamoto warrior family established itself as Japan's military and civil rulers after defeating the Taira clan in the Gempei War (1180-1185). This defeat marked the end of the 25-year Taira reign. The rise of the Minamoto led to a Japanese warrior government called a shogunate in 1192, and increased the power of Japan's hereditary warriors called samurai. The Minamoto have governed effectively, which offers a chance for national stability.
