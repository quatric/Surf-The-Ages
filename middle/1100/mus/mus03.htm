Accommodations
 

Our pilgrimage assistance is designed to meet your spiritual and physical needs. Each part of your journey is carefully planned to ensure your safe passage to Mecca and the fulfillment of your duty. We provide:

• The best routes

• Stops to accommodate prayer times

• Fine accommodations for pilgrims, both on the road and at Mecca

• Food and water at all stops

Side trips to other holy cities, such as Jerusalem and Medina, may be included in the itinerary.

Special assistance is given to Muslims who decide to settle in Mecca.
