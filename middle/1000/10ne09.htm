9. Ghana Empire in turmoil

The Soninke people, who had controlled the Ghana Empire for 300 years, were conquered by Berber Almoravids from North Africa in the middle of the 1000's. Later in the century, the Soninkes retook their historic capital, Kumbi Saleh, but were unable to recapture the outer parts of their former empire. The famous Ghana Empire, which has been a major trading partner with northern Africa and other parts of the Arab world, may never regain its former glory.
