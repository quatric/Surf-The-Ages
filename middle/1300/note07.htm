7. African emperor's travels bring glory to Mali

In 1324, Mansa Musa, ruler of the Mali Empire, dazzled the world with a seemingly inexhaustible supply of gold on his pilgrimage to Mecca, the holiest city of Islam. Mansa Musa also journeyed to Cairo. His travels bolstered regional trade relations and encouraged architects and other learned people to come to Mali. These efforts have helped make Mali the political and cultural leader of West Africa.
