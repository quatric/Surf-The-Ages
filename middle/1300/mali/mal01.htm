Welcome to The Mali News. In this 1324 edition, we spotlight the visit of Mali's illustrious ruler, Mansa Musa, to Cairo. This year, the powerful and devout Mansa Musa graced the city of Cairo with his royal presence while on his pilgrimage to the holy city of Mecca. As part of our coverage of this remarkable event, which has spread the fame of Mali throughout the civilized world, The Mali News spoke to residents of Cairo. As expected, Cairenes were properly overwhelmed by our glorious king and his beneficence, which was unlike anything they had ever been privileged to experience.
