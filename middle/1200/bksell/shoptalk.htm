All sales and rentals are in accord with regulations governing booksellers established by the University of Paris.

Commissions for special illuminated copies accepted. Jacques de Bretagne employs both scribes and illuminators. Noble patrons desiring quality bindings may supply their own jewels and fine leathers or purchase them through arrangements with our recommended dealers.

Scriptorium and sales located in the Latin Quarter of Paris near the Cathedral of Notre Dame.

Also visit Jacques’s conveniently located sales booths on several Paris bridges.
Used books--buy or sell. Need cash? Let Jacques sell your old books on commission.
Warning: New titles brought from outside the City of Paris must be approved by the university authorities before being offered for sale.

U-Copy services available. Faculty and students may rent books in order to make their own copies.

Money back guarantee: If anyone copying one of our books finds the book to be incomplete or copied inaccurately, Jacques de Bretagne will pay the borrower damages for the time wasted in copying a faulty text, according to university regulations.
