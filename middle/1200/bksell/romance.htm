Love Murder Revenge
Kriemhild, the beautiful sister of King Gunther of Burgundy, is wooed and wed by the bold warrior Siegfried. But her passionate marriage is not destined to last. When Gunther’s wife, Brünhild, learns a terrible secret, vicious plots lead to the murder of Kriemhild’s beloved husband. No matter how long it takes, Kriemhild’s bloody vengeance is sure to follow.

Will noble young Parzival reach the Holy Grail? Or will temptation lure him from his quest? Wolfram von Eschenbach’s runaway hit of the 1200’s.

Three romances about the court of King Arthur from French poet Chrétien de Troyes. Written in the late 1100’s and copied again by popular demand, with modern illuminations.
