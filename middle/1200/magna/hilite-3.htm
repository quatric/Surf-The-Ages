Taxes

The King will not impose new inheritance taxes. New taxes shall be levied for only three reasons.
  • First, if the King is captured in battle and the barons agree to pay the ransom.
  • Second, to fund celebrations of the knighting of the King's eldest son.
  • Third, to fund the marriage of the King's eldest daughter.
In addition, the barons may collect new taxes from their people only under the same three conditions.
