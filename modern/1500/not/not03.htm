3. Protestant churches arise in Europe

A movement to reform the Roman Catholic Church has led to the rise of new churches in many parts of Europe. Early in the 1500's, many Roman Catholics withdrew from the church because it failed to address profound disagreements about religious beliefs and criticisms of church practices outlined by reform leader Martin Luther. Followers of Luther's ideas, known as Protestants, have established Protestant churches throughout northern Europe.
