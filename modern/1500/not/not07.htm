7. Mughal Empire takes over much of southern Asia

Mughal leader Babur established the Mughal Empire in southern Asia in 1526. His grandson, Akbar, later set up its governmental structure. The Mughal Empire has brought a new level of order to the region through its centralized government. Akbar has proven to be a wise ruler. Although he himself is Muslim, he has won the loyalty of Hindus through his religious tolerance.
