William Shakespeare, London's rising star

London is all abuzz over the plays of William Shakespeare. His Richard III, A Midsummer Night's Dream, and Romeo and Juliet have established Shakespeare in the premier ranks of London’s playwrights. English clergyman and schoolmaster Francis Meres has said of Shakespeare in his recent book Palladis Tamia: Wit's Treasury, "As Plautus and Seneca are accounted the best for Comedy and Tragedy among the Latins: so Shakespeare among the English is the most excellent in both kinds for the stage."

Shakespeare is also a poet and an actor as well as a playwright. His long poem "Venus and Adonis" was an immediate success. Shakespeare is currently a member of the Lord Chamberlain’s Men, one of the most popular acting companies in London. Be it through a fine performance on stage, or through his characterizations and lively scripts, Shakespeare is sure to please everyone from the refined gentleman to the groundling in the yard clamoring for a swordfight.
