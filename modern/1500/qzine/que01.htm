Queen Elizabeth is the daughter of King Henry VIII and his second wife, Anne Boleyn. Elizabeth, who was born in 1533, has ruled since 1558. Her reign has been a time of great achievement and prosperity for England.

Mary Stuart, currently awaiting execution at the Tower of London, became Queen of Scotland in 1542. After numerous scandals, including the murder of her husband, Mary was forced to abdicate her throne in 1567. She has been involved in numerous plots to overthrow or assassinate Queen Elizabeth.
