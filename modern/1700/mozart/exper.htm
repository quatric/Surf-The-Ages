I have played the royal courts of Europe since the age of 6 and am a prolific composer. If you enjoy the music of Bach (J. S. and J. C.) and Haydn, my work will please you.

My creativity and productivity are such that, even during times when I am inexplicably without patronage, I have created exceptional crowd-pleasers. These include the opera The Marriage of Figaro and Symphony No. 41. My latest work is the opera The Magic Flute, being performed to rave reviews.
