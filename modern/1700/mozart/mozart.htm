Composer/musician Wolfgang Amadeus Mozart seeks students, commissions, and performance opportunities. I will teach piano to noble ladies or gentlemen (or persons of lesser social standing but equivalent or better means) or will write music of any kind to your specifications (vocal compositions in German or Italian).

All those seeking lessons, custom compositions, a sensitive accompanist, stylish soloist, or ensemble performer should inquire at the Mozart home in Vienna. Ask for Wolfgang or leave a message with Constanza.
