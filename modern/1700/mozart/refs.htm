Holy Roman Empress Maria Theresa of Austria
I ordered a serenade from Mozart as a wedding present for my son Ferdinand. It brought down the house!

Holy Roman Emperor Joseph II of Austria
I most enjoyed Mozart's operas and attended many of his concerts before I became his patron. This young man is destined to rank among the immortals!

Joseph Haydn
Before God and as an honest man, I tell you that Mozart is the greatest composer known to me either in person or by name.
