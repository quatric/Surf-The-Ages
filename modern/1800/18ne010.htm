10. Success of Great Exhibition leads to more world fairs

London held a magnificent fair in 1851 where exciting innovations in art, handicrafts, and machinery were exhibited. The fair, called the Great Exhibition, was housed in the Crystal Palace, a revolutionary glass and iron structure. The success of the fair prompted other major international exhibitions in the latter half of the century, notably a fair in Paris in 1889 and an exposition in Chicago in 1893.
