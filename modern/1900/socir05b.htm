Joyner-Kersee, Jackie (1962- ), an American athlete, became the first woman to win consecutive heptathlon championships at the Olympic Games. The heptathlon is a seven-event track and field competition held over two days. Joyner-Kersee won the Olympic heptathlon in 1988 and 1992, setting a world record of 7,291 points in the 1988 games. Joyner-Kersee had finished second in the heptathlon in the 1984 Olympics. Joyner-Kersee also won the Olympic long jump in 1988, setting an Olympic record of 24 feet 3.5 inches (7.40 meters). She finished third in the long jump in the 1992 and 1996 games.
