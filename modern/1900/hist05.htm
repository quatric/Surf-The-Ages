5. The development of the transistor and related improvements (middle to late 1900's)

Invented in 1947, the transistor began the process of miniaturizing the components of communications systems and spread from there to other areas. Today, transistors--combined in the form of microprocessors--operate communications satellites, cellular telephones, missiles, ATM machines, computers, CD players, automobiles, and nearly every modern electronic device.
