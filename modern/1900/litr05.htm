5. The U.S. stock market crash (1929)

In its immediate context, the crash led to the great world depression that fostered the economic conditions for World War II (1939-1945). But the long-range ramifications of the crash included the worldwide legislation that tempered capitalism with social welfare. The socially conscious literature, art, and philosophy of the 1930's had at its base the crash of 1929 and the Great Depression that followed.
