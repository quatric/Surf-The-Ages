Nathaniel Hawthorne's classic book The Scarlet Letter portrays the world of Puritan New England. This site explores Hawthorne's work and links to related Hawthorne material.

SCIENCE

The Galileo Project
http://es.rice.edu:80/ES/humsoc/Galileo/
The Galileo Project is a source of information on the life and work of Galileo Galilei and the science of his time. This site, prepared by Rice University, contains links to Internet resources for modern astronomy and astrophysics.

American Memory-- Front Door
http://memory.loc.gov/
The American Memory Project is an archival resource for exploring many aspects of American history and culture.

South African Cultural History Museum
http://users.iafrica.com/g/gr/grenvill/museum.htm
The South African Cultural History Museum's collections are housed in a number of buildings situated in and around Cape Town. They represent the cultures of peoples from around the world, from antiquity to the present, as well as aspects of Cape Town history.
